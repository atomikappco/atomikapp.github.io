# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults 25–40 who have read (or know about) "Hábitos Atómicos" by James Clear and are actively looking for an app that applies that specific philosophy — not a generic habit tracker. They arrive at the landing already conceptually aligned with the framework; the landing must confirm that ATOMIK is the faithful implementation they were looking for. They are bilingual (Spanish primary, English secondary). They are skeptical of hype, value honesty over motivation, and respond to data and clarity over promises.

## Product Purpose

ATOMIK is an iOS habit tracker built around the identity-based habit framework from James Clear's Atomic Habits. It tracks streaks, shows real progress data, awards milestones at scientifically grounded intervals, and links each habit to an identity declaration. Success looks like a user downloading the app within 60 seconds of landing, convinced that this is the only habit app that actually gets the philosophy right.

## Brand Personality

Serena · Científica · Honesta

Voice: calm and confident, never loud. Language is specific and direct — no motivational filler. The product speaks the user's language (Spanish first). It shows data without editorializing. It celebrates consistency without overselling it.

## Anti-references

- Gamified productivity apps (Habitica): avatars, XP points, cartoon aesthetics, RPG tone
- Aggressive mascot apps (Duolingo): streak shaming, loud notifications design, bright toy-like colors
- Generic AI SaaS landing pages: purple/blue hero gradients, enormous "hero metric" numbers, copy that says "unleash", "seamless", "transform", "next-gen"
- Generic fitness apps: stock photos of people sweating, green/orange gradients, exaggerated before/after promises

## Design Principles

1. **Show the philosophy, don't describe it.** Every section should demonstrate Atomic Habits concepts visually, not just caption them. The streak grid IS the "never miss twice" rule. The identity cards ARE the identity-based framework.
2. **Earn trust with honesty.** Real data, organic-looking numbers, no round figures or fake round-up stats. If the app is new, the sparse bar chart is honest — don't fake it.
3. **Stillness over stimulation.** No aggressive motion, no popups, no urgency tactics. The design should feel like the book feels: calm, methodical, intelligent.
4. **Spanish-first, English-ready.** Default language is Spanish. The EN toggle is a courtesy, not the primary experience.
5. **One idea per section.** No section tries to do two things. Streak section = one principle. Identity section = one principle. The focus is the feature.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Bilingual (ES/EN) with runtime switching — all text must be localizable
- Support reduced-motion preference (no animation-dependent content visibility)
- Keyboard navigable (focus indicators required)
- Color contrast: body text ≥ 4.5:1, large text ≥ 3:1
